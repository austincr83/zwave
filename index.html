A mock-up of your background with the current widget placed at the top, to check that text doesn't collide with the clock or icons. The icons are only for show — Export here saves just your wallpaper.

How to use your creations
  1. Wallpaper: Export, save the image to Photos, then in Photos tap Share → Use as Wallpaper.
  2. Widget image: Export and save to Photos. Put it in its own album, then long-press the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Photos, and choose that album.
  3. Live widget: Copy the Scriptable script, paste it into a new script in the Scriptable app, then add a Scriptable widget and pick the script.
  4. Install this app: In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen so it opens full screen and works offline.
